00 / MusikoMP3 player for Windows

A soundtrack that’s yours.

Musiko is a lightweight, translucent MP3 player for Windows. Add your songs, press play, and keep your library on your own PC.

Download for WindowsOpen in browser

01 / FeaturesSix things it does

Everything for your own music files

Select Add music or drag audio files onto the window. Musiko supports MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A and OGG.

Lyrics

Synced lyrics

Reads lyrics embedded in your MP3s or a matching .lrc file. When timed lyrics are missing, Musiko looks them up online.

Tags

Titles and cover art

Title, artist, album and embedded cover artwork are picked up from your files automatically.

Library

Favorites and playlists

Save tracks with the heart, build playlists, or let Auto Mix put a set together. Press / to search by title, artist or album.

Player

Full-screen mode

Open the immersive player with the expand button. Shuffle, repeat, skip and set the volume from the bar at the bottom.

Keys

Keyboard friendly

Space plays and pauses. Arrow keys jump five seconds. Ctrl+B collapses the sidebar.

Private

Stays on your PC

Musiko reads files locally and does not upload them. Your library, favorites and playlists are stored on this PC.

04 / InstallThree steps

Up and playing in a minute

Musiko runs in its own app window on Windows. You can also use it in a browser on any computer.

Windows PCMicrosoft Edge installedFree

  1. Download Musiko

    Download the zip file and extract it to a folder you like.

  2. Open Musiko.exe

    Double-click Musiko.exe. The player opens in its own Microsoft Edge app window.

  3. Add shortcuts (optional)

    Double-click Install-Musiko.bat to create Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts.
